1. The Service

HeadWell is a consumer wellness app for individuals with recurring headache patterns (chronic or episodic migraine, tension-type, cluster, vestibular, sinus, or other recurrent headache disorders). The Service allows you to:

  • Log headache attacks and prodrome events with clinical-grade detail.
  • Track menstrual cycles, life events, and lifestyle factors.
  • Receive a daily personalized risk forecast based on barometric pressure, sleep, HRV, cycle phase, and your own history.
  • Complete validated clinical assessments (MIDAS, HIT-6, ACE-10).
  • Use guided breathing, stretching, and craniofacial massage protocols.
  • Generate doctor-ready PDF summary reports.

Wellness, not medicine

Always consult a licensed clinician for any health concern. If you are experiencing a sudden, severe, or entirely new type of headache, seek emergency medical attention.
