What HeadWell does that others don't

Daily risk forecast

Concrete percentages (32%, not "moderate") backed by your iPhone's barometer and Apple WeatherKit. 7-day outlook updates every morning.

Patterns from your own data

12 personalized insight cards unlock as your data matures — sleep deficit, perimenstrual clusters, weather sensitivity, day-of-week patterns.

Active relief, not just tracking

Three evidence-based intervention modalities — breathing, cervical stretches, craniofacial massage — guided on-device.

Doctor-ready PDF reports

One tap generates a clinical summary with narrative, trigger frequency, medication effectiveness, and HealthKit correlations — no PII.

Anatomically precise logging

19-region head map. Left, right, or both. Plain labels (Forehead, Behind eyes, Temples) — no clinical jargon.

Privacy-first by design

No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Apple Health stays on your device — only daily aggregates are ever stored.
