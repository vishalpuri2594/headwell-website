Frequently asked questions

Why does HeadWell need an account?

Your health data is private. An account scopes everything to you and lets you restore your record on a new device. We support Sign in with Google or email on every phone, and Sign in with Apple on iPhone and iPad.

Why does it ask for health data access?

HeadWell can read daily sleep, heart rate, HRV, and step count (from Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android) to spot patterns linked to your headaches. Raw Health samples never leave your device — we only store daily aggregates (one sleep number, one HRV number). You can use HeadWell fully without it; the forecast just has less to go on.

Why location and barometer?

Some people find that changes in barometric pressure bring on their headaches, so HeadWell can look at the pressure where you are. It uses your phone's pressure sensor where it has one, and your location once per refresh to fetch a local forecast from a weather service. Location is not stored.

How accurate is the risk forecast?

HeadWell's forecast is a personalized score derived from your own logged data and publicly available weather data. It is not a clinical prediction or diagnostic tool. Like any pattern-based system, it gets sharper as you log more entries.

How do I export my data?

Open the app → Profile → App → Export Data. You can export everything as CSV (for spreadsheets) or JSON (for backup). Files are generated on your device and shared via your phone's share sheet — they never touch our servers.

How do I delete my account?

On Android, open the app → Profile → App → Delete Account. On iPhone and iPad, email us from your account address (see Delete your account). Deletion is irreversible and removes all your data from our backend within 30 days.

How much does HeadWell cost?

HeadWell is free. If we introduce optional paid features later, you'll be notified before any charge and existing features won't be retroactively gated.

Will my doctor accept the PDF report?

The doctor PDF is designed for neurologists, headache specialists, and primary care providers. It includes a summary, how often attacks happen, trigger patterns, and your MIDAS and HIT-6 scores if you have completed them, formatted for a clinical visit. It does not include your name or email. Whether your clinician finds it useful depends on them.

Is HeadWell HIPAA-compliant?

HeadWell is a consumer wellness app, not a Covered Entity under HIPAA. We follow HIPAA-style best practices (TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest, least-privilege access, audit logging) but we do not market the app as HIPAA-compliant. We are not a Business Associate to any healthcare provider.

Does HeadWell work offline?

Most logging and on-device features work offline. Risk forecasting needs internet for weather and barometer cross-checks. Your offline logs sync the next time the app comes online.

I have a feature suggestion or bug to report

Email headeasesupport@gmail.com with as much detail as you can. Screenshots help. We read every message.
