Delete Your HeadWell Account

You can permanently delete your HeadWell account and all data associated with it at any time. Deletion is irreversible — there is no recovery or grace-period restore. If you want to keep a copy of your data, export it first (in the app: Profile → App → Export Data, as CSV or JSON).

Option 1 — Delete in the Android app (fastest)

Open HeadWell → Profile tab → expand the App section → Delete Account → confirm. Your account, all data synced to our servers, and all data on the device are erased immediately.

On iPhone and iPad, use Option 2 below; it takes one email.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email (iPhone, iPad, or no app needed)

If you use HeadWell on iPhone or iPad, have uninstalled the app, or can't sign in, email us from the address associated with your account:

Email: headeasesupport@gmail.com with the subject line "Account deletion request".

We will verify the request via a reply to your account email (to protect your data from third-party deletion requests) and complete the deletion within 30 days, typically much sooner. We will confirm by email once it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, display name, sign-in identity).
  • All headache, prodrome, period, and life-event logs.
  • Intake and follow-up survey answers; MIDAS, HIT-6, and ACE assessment results.
  • Patient details (address, insurance, care team) if you entered them.
  • Daily health aggregates from Apple Health / Health Connect (sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, steps).
  • Activity sessions, points, achievements, preferences, and sync records.
  • All active sign-in sessions and tokens.

What is retained

  • Security audit entries that contain no personal data — your email is stored only as a one-way hash in these records, which we keep to protect the service against abuse.
  • Backend request logs, which rotate automatically after 30 days.
  • Nothing else. We do not keep "deactivated" copies of your data.

Details on everything we collect and why are in our Privacy Policy.
